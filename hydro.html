Who is Norsk Hydro?

Before the attack of March 2019, Hydro was already one of the world's most integrated aluminum and renewable-energy companies, a global industrial giant rooted in Norway.

Norsk Hydro · Oslo, Norway
  • Global aluminum leader Founded in 1905, Norsk Hydro is one of the world's largest aluminum companies, operating in 40+ countries with roughly 35,000 employees across mining, smelting, and finished products.
